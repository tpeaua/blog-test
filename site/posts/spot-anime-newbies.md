# The Starter Pack

You know the type. They discovered anime last Tuesday, but suddenly they've "been into it for years." Here's how to spot them before they get through their first "dattebayo."

## Telltale Phrases

- "Oh, you probably haven't heard of Attack on Titan. It's super underground."
- "I prefer subs, obviously. The Japanese voice acting is just more... emotional." (They once watched a single subtitled episode of Naruto.)
- "Kawaii desu!" delivered with the confidence of someone who learned Japanese from a TikTok.
- "I've been a fan for a long time—like, since the pandemic started."

## The Veteran Litmus Test

Ask them to name a manga that isn't currently being adapted into a hit anime. Wait for the silence.

## Quick Diagnostic

```
if favorite_anime in ['Attack on Titan', 'Demon Slayer', 'Jujutsu Kaisen'] and years_claimed > years_watched:
    print('Welcome, newbie.')
elif can_explain_filler and has_survived_a_hiatus:
    print('Okay, you may sit with us.')
```

## Bottom Line

Everyone starts somewhere. Just don't lecture us on "peak fiction" after completing one season and sitting through a single Crunchyroll ad.
